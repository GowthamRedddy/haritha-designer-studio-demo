About the Studio

Design that balances tradition & today

Haritha Designer Studio creates customised couture — bridal kurta sets, half sarees, festive lehengas, family ensembles and kidswear — balancing contemporary design with traditional craft.

Customers across India and abroad order with us by sharing their design references on WhatsApp — from bridal outfits to dhoti ceremony wear — and we stitch to their measurements and ship worldwide. Every outfit is designed with the client, one at a time.
